# Tzahaynesh Adel-Krut — personal site

The personal professional website of a psychotherapist, supervisor and lecturer, built
as a single-practitioner site separate from the two-person venture she worked under.
This glossary fixes the vocabulary; copy that contradicts it is wrong however well it reads.

## Language

### People

**צהיינש אדל־קרוט / Tzahaynesh Adel-Krut**:
The sole subject of the site. Psychotherapist, clinical supervisor, lecturer and workshop
facilitator; private clinic in Hoshaya.
_Avoid_: Tzahinash, Tzahaynesh Adel Krut, Adel Krut

**תמר / Tamar**:
Co-founder with her of the joint venture. Not a subject of this site and not named on it.

### Practice

**ענווה תרבותית / Cultural Humility**:
A stance toward meeting any person — curiosity toward their story, identity, values and
belongings, together with willingness to examine one's own assumptions and the power
relations in the encounter. A way of meeting difference, never knowledge about particular
populations.
_Avoid_: מודעות בינתרבותית, intercultural awareness, cultural competence, cultural sensitivity

**עבודה בינתרבותית / Intercultural work**:
The applied practice that draws on cultural humility. One significant domain of her work,
never the whole of it.

**טיפול / Therapy**:
Individual psychotherapy with adolescents and adults. CBT integrated with ACT, mindfulness
and drama-therapy tools.
_Avoid_: ייעוץ, coaching, treatment

### The three senses of הדרכה

Hebrew uses one root for three services with different audiences. Unqualified **הדרכה**
is ambiguous and must not appear as a navigation label or page title.

**הדרכה קלינית / Clinical supervision**:
Professional oversight of a therapist's clinical work, individually or in a group. Audience
is therapists and therapy teams. She is a registered supervisor (מדריכה רשומה).
_Avoid_: bare הדרכה, mentoring, guidance

**הדרכת הורים / Parent guidance**:
Work with parents on their own parenting. The parent is the client; the child is not in
treatment. Audience is parents.
_Avoid_: bare הדרכה, parent coaching, ייעוץ הורי, family therapy

**הוראה / Teaching**:
Academic and programme instruction — courses and teaching units inside a training
programme. Audience is students.
_Avoid_: bare הדרכה, הכשרה

### Adjacent terms

**ייעוץ / Consultation**:
Advisory work for a professional or an organization on a case or a situation. Distinct from
therapy (no client relationship) and from supervision (no ongoing responsibility for
practice).

**הכשרה / Training**:
A structured programme for professionals — a workshop, learning day or series. Distinct
from הוראה, which sits inside an academic curriculum.

**מסלול / Track**:
One of the routes the site offers a visitor. Tracks organise the site by what someone came
for, not by who they are.

### The joint venture

**דרך ענווה / Derech Anava**:
The two-person consulting and training venture she ran with Tamar, described on its own
public site as founded by both of them. Its name does not appear on this site; the body of
work does. See `docs/adr/0001-omitting-the-joint-venture-name.md`.
