# Omitting the joint venture's name

Her biography covers roughly twenty years of intercultural development, consulting and
training carried out under a two-person venture, דרך ענווה. The commissioner's brief is
that this site is separate and does not name it; that instruction was reaffirmed after the
conflict below was put to him. We therefore keep the body of work in the biography and omit
only the brand name, isolating the affected sentence in a single content field so the
decision can be reversed with one edit.

## Consequences

Her own questionnaire answers contradict the brief. She describes herself as the venture's
*founder and manager* in the present tense and first person singular, lists it first among
her professional stations, offers its shared address as a contact email for this site, and
names its website as the design reference she likes. The venture's live public site credits
two women as co-founders (*מייסדות*).

Two risks follow. Publishing her as sole founder of something a live page credits to two
people is a dispute waiting to be indexed, so the wording must be confirmed with her before
launch rather than inferred from the brief. And if the joint site stays up carrying her
biography, both pages will surface for her name, making the separation visible to anyone who
searches — which is a decision about the old site, not something this site can solve.
