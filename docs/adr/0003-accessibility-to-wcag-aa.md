# Building to WCAG 2.0 AA

Israeli regulations require businesses serving the public to make their websites accessible
(IS 5568, which adopts WCAG 2.0 AA) and to publish an accessibility statement. Whether a
private therapy practice falls in scope is a question for a qualified adviser and has not
been confirmed. We are building to AA regardless and shipping a הצהרת נגישות page.

## Consequences

On a static Hebrew content site, meeting AA from the outset costs very little — semantic
markup, sufficient contrast, visible keyboard focus, genuine alt text, correct heading
order, and no meaning carried by colour alone. Retrofitting it later is expensive and
usually partial. Treating it as a build constraint rather than a legal question avoids
having to answer the legal question under deadline.
