# Five tracks, not four

She summarised her own work as four services twice, in two different ways: one list pairs
therapy with parent guidance, teaching and intercultural lectures; the other pairs therapy
with clinical supervision, training and organisational work. Parent guidance appears only in
the first, clinical supervision only in the second. We split the difference into five tracks
— טיפול, הדרכת הורים, הדרכה וייעוץ למטפלים, הרצאות והכשרות, ארגונים ואקדמיה — because both
services are real and each has an audience the other does not.

## Consequences

The site carries one more top-level route than its subject asked for, so a future reader
comparing the nav against her own words will find an extra item. That is deliberate.

The deciding factor is that Hebrew uses **הדרכה** both for supervising therapists and for
guiding parents. Folding the two into one track would put a parent looking for help with
their adolescent onto a page written for clinicians. She ranked parents first among her
audiences, which makes that the more costly mistake. For the same reason, bare **הדרכה**
must never be used as a navigation label or page title — it is ambiguous exactly where the
visitor is choosing which way to go.
